# Sentinel Bank Fee Waiver Policy

1. Customer Service Agents are authorized to waive late fees and minor service charges for valid reasons (e.g., medical emergency, bank error, first-time offense).
2. The maximum allowable fee waiver amount per transaction is $50.00.
3. Any fee waiver exceeding $50.00 requires a manager's override and MUST NOT be processed by an autonomous agent.
4. Agents must always document the 'reason' clearly.
